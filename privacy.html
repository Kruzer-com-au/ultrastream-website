01 · Overview

Introduction

Kruzer Ultraverse ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and safeguard personal information through the UltraStream mobile application (the "App"), and the rights you have in relation to that information.

This policy is designed to meet the requirements of the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA/CPRA), applicable app store requirements (Apple App Store and Google Play), and applicable global privacy laws and best practice. Where local laws apply to you, the rights and protections in those laws are in addition to the general protections set out in this policy.

By downloading, installing, registering for an account on, or otherwise using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This policy is a companion to the Kruzer Ultraverse website Privacy Policy. Where you also use our websites, that policy applies to your use of the websites.

Some features described in this policy, including age verification and reward checkout, are being progressively rolled out and may not yet be fully active for all users. Where a feature is not yet active, we do not process the related personal information until it goes live.

02 · Controller

Who we are

Kruzer Ultraverse is the operator of the App and acts as the "data controller" (under GDPR/UK GDPR), "APP entity" (under the Australian Privacy Act), and "business" (under the CCPA/CPRA) responsible for your personal information.

For any privacy-related questions, requests, or complaints, please contact:

Privacy contact: admin@kruzer.com.au

Postal address: Kruzer Ultraverse Pty Ltd (ABN 62 686 962 783), Level 13, 60 Castlereagh Street, Sydney NSW 2000, Australia

If you are in the EU or UK and require an Article 27 representative, please contact us at the address above and we will provide current details.

03 · Scope

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the UltraStream mobile application and the services accessed through it, including creating an account, live streaming, viewing streams, chatting, participating in the Founding Creator Programme, and receiving creator payouts. It does not apply to third-party websites or services linked from or integrated with the App; those third parties have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review.

04 · Collection

Personal information we collect

We collect personal information in the following categories:

4.1 Account and profile information

When you create an account or sign in to the App, we collect information such as:

  • Your name, display name or username, and custom handle
  • Email address
  • Password (stored in hashed form: we never store plain-text passwords)
  • Where you sign up or sign in via Google or Apple, the identifier and basic profile information (such as name and email) shared with us by that provider
  • Profile information you choose to provide, such as a profile picture or bio
  • Account preferences and settings
  • Authentication metadata (for example, sign-in timestamps, IP address used to sign in, and approximate location derived from IP for security purposes)

4.2 Age verification

To determine which content is appropriate to show you, the App uses a video-based age verification process provided by our third-party verification partner, Yoti. Where this feature is active:

  • Yoti processes a short video or image of you to estimate your age. This is biometric information.
  • Kruzer Ultraverse receives an age tier or age confirmation result from Yoti, not the underlying video or image itself, which is handled directly by Yoti in accordance with its own privacy policy and retention practices
  • The age tier is used to determine which content categories are visible to your account.

We rely on your explicit consent to carry out age estimation, which is a form of biometric processing. You may withdraw consent at any time, and we offer an alternative age-verification method where estimation is unavailable or declined. We complete a Data Protection Impact Assessment before this feature is enabled.

This feature is in development and not yet enforced in the current release.

4.3 Creator payout, purchases, and financial information

If you join the App as a creator and are eligible for revenue share, we (and our payment processor, Stripe) collect information necessary to verify your identity and pay you, including:

  • Full legal name and date of birth
  • Bank account or payout details
  • Tax information (for example, a tax file number or equivalent, as required by law)
  • Identity verification documents required by Stripe for Know Your Customer (KYC) checks
  • Earnings, payout history, and referral or competition activity relevant to the Founding Creator Programme

If you reward a creator using Squad Legacy items, we (and Stripe) also collect information needed to process that purchase, including:

  • Payment details processed by Stripe at checkout
  • A record of the items and quantities purchased, and the creator rewarded
  • Your contribution and transaction history, visible to you in the App's Transaction Log

Payment card and full bank account details are collected and stored by Stripe on our behalf. We do not store full card numbers or full bank account numbers ourselves.

Reward checkout via Stripe is in development and not yet processable end-to-end in the current release.

4.4 Device permissions and device information

To provide live streaming, the App requests access to certain device features. We only access these when needed to provide the relevant functionality, and you can manage permissions at any time through your device settings:

  • Camera: to capture and broadcast your live video stream
  • Microphone: to capture and broadcast your live audio
  • Photo/video library: where you choose to upload a profile picture, thumbnail, or pre-recorded clip
  • Push notification token: to send you alerts about your stream, followers, and account activity
  • Device identifiers, operating system, app version, and crash/diagnostic data: to keep the App secure and working properly

If you do not grant camera or microphone permission, you will not be able to broadcast a live stream, but you can still use other parts of the App.

4.5 Streaming content and activity

When you use the App to stream, watch, or interact with content, we collect:

  • Your live video and audio stream while broadcasting, and any short-form or uploaded video content
  • Chat messages, comments, likes, dislikes, and reactions you post
  • Follows, subscriptions, and other interactions with creators and viewers
  • Watch history, including videos, Shorts, and live content you view. You can pause watch history tracking or clear your watch history at any time in the App

Live streams may be recorded and stored so they can be replayed as video-on-demand where you enable that option. You can delete your recordings at any time in the App. We keep recordings while published and for up to 30 days after removal, unless we must retain them longer to investigate a report or comply with law. Creators can choose not to store recordings in stream settings.

4.6 App analytics and technical information

When you use the App, we and our analytics providers automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Pages/screens viewed and features used within the App
  • Date, time, and duration of sessions
  • Clickstream and in-app interaction data

This information is collected using reputable third-party analytics, crash-reporting, and content-delivery providers, who are contractually required to handle it consistently with this policy.

4.7 Support, contact forms, and communications

When you contact us through in-app support, a contact form, or otherwise communicate with us, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Any other information you choose to include in your message
  • Subscription preferences and email engagement data, if you sign up to receive updates

4.8 Information we do not knowingly collect

Other than the age-verification biometric processing described in Section 4.2 above, we do not knowingly collect "sensitive information" (as defined under the Australian Privacy Act) or "special category" data (as defined under the GDPR), such as health information, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, political opinions, or sexual orientation, unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, in a support message) and it is necessary for the purpose of your enquiry.

05 · Sources

How we collect personal information

  • Directly from you, when you register, log in, set up creator payouts, stream, chat, or otherwise interact with the App
  • Automatically, through device permissions, app analytics, and diagnostic tools when you use the App
  • From third-party service providers, such as Stripe (payments), authentication providers, analytics platforms, and referral/competition tracking tools, in accordance with their own privacy policies
06 · Purpose

Why we use your personal information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you
  • Provide live streaming, chat, and viewing functionality
  • Verify your age and determine which content categories are appropriate to show you
  • Process reward purchases and calculate, process, and pay creator revenue share through Stripe
  • Run the Founding Creator Programme, including referral tracking and eligible competitions
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App, including troubleshooting and security
  • Communicate with you about your account, support requests, and (where you have opted in) marketing updates
  • Comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations, including identity verification for payouts

We will not use your personal information for a purpose materially different from the purpose for which it was collected without first notifying you and, where required, obtaining your consent.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our lawful bases are: performance of our contract with you (account, streaming, payouts); your consent (age estimation, optional marketing, non-essential tracking); our legitimate interests (security, product improvement, fraud prevention), balanced against your rights; and compliance with legal obligations (tax, KYC/AML, safety). You can withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it.

08 · Sharing

Disclosure of personal information

We may disclose personal information to:

  • Stripe, to process creator payouts, reward purchases, and carry out identity verification
  • Yoti, to carry out video-based age verification (once this feature is active)
  • Google and Apple, where you choose to sign up or sign in using those providers
  • Service providers who help us operate the App, including hosting, streaming infrastructure, authentication, analytics, push notifications, customer support tools, and security providers. These providers are contractually required to handle personal information only as instructed by us and consistent with this policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA. We will update this policy and provide a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" control if that ever changes.

Marketing communications

Unsubscribing from marketing communications does not affect transactional or service-related communications, such as account security notices, payout confirmations, or important changes to the App or these terms.

11 · Security

Data security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational security measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest where appropriate
  • Hashing of passwords using industry-standard algorithms
  • Payment and identity verification data handled by Stripe under its own PCI-compliant security standards
  • Access controls and authentication requirements for our personnel
  • Regular review of our security practices and service providers

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or information has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, and other regulators where applicable.

13 · Your rights

Your privacy rights

Subject to the laws that apply to you, you may have the following rights in relation to your personal information:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction / rectification: ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Deletion / erasure: ask us to delete personal information in certain circumstances
  • Restriction / objection: ask us to restrict or object to certain processing
  • Data portability: request a copy of certain personal information in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal)
  • Opt out of marketing: opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time
  • Opt out of "sale" or "sharing": under the CCPA/CPRA. We do not sell or share personal information as defined under that law, but if this changes we will provide a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" option
  • Non-discrimination: exercise your rights without being discriminated against for doing so
  • Watch history controls: you can pause watch history tracking or clear your watch history at any time in the App, under Profile

Some information, such as payout and tax records, may need to be retained even after a deletion request in order to meet our legal obligations.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at admin@kruzer.com.au. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (typically within 30 days under the GDPR/UK GDPR; 45 days under the CCPA/CPRA, with the possibility of an extension).

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority:

  • Australia: the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): oaic.gov.au
  • EU: your local Data Protection Authority
  • UK: the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk
  • California: the California Privacy Protection Agency or the California Attorney General

We would, however, appreciate the chance to address your concerns first.

14 · Minors

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at admin@kruzer.com.au and we will take steps to delete that information.

While Viewers must be at least 18, Creators who receive payouts must be at least 18. We do not knowingly enable payouts to anyone under 18.

Automated decision-making

We use automated tools to help verify age, recommend content, and detect content or conduct that may breach our Terms or the law. These tools support human review; we do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on you based solely on automated processing without a route to human review. Where a moderation or account decision significantly affects you, for example removing content or restricting payouts, you can ask us to review it at admin@kruzer.com.au.
